# Code of Conduct

This project is maintained for practical collaboration around reusable Shopify
Polaris-style chart components.

We expect participants to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Assume good intent while still being precise about bugs and risks.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or disruptive behavior.

Maintainers may close issues, hide comments, or restrict participation when
behavior makes the project harder or less safe to use.
